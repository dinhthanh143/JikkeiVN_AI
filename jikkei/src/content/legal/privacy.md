## Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Jikkei handles account data, usage data, and platform interactions.

## Data We Collect

- Account details such as email address, username, and authentication state.
- Content you submit, save, or generate while using the platform.
- Technical data needed for security, debugging, session management, and service reliability.

## How We Use Data

- To create and secure accounts.
- To provide requested features and personalized experiences.
- To detect abuse, enforce policies, and maintain platform integrity.

## Cookies and Sessions

Jikkei uses secure session cookies for authentication. These cookies support login, refresh, and legal-consent enforcement.

## Data Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for service operation, legal compliance, security, or legitimate business purposes.

## Your Choices

You may review or update account details through the platform where supported. You can also contact support if you need help with privacy-related requests.
